Movie Review: Sunrise

Sunrise is an inspiring coming-of-age drama about Atlas, a young heir who defies his family's expectations to pursue his dream of becoming a musician. With his loyal friends Leah and John by his side, Atlas must overcome self-doubt, family opposition, and the pressures of fame. A heartfelt story about finding your voice and the courage to rise beyond what others expect of you.
